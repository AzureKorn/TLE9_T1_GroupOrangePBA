Overview

Our Chosen Problem

Out of the 3 identified problems, which are: unequal access to technology, misinformation or fake media, and lack of personal data privacy, the problem we chose as our main problem to focus on is "Misinformation or Fake Media".

What is it?

Misinformation or Fake Media is intentionally spreading false or misleading information to deceive people online. People may believe this false information and share it to others without checking if it's true or factual.

Who is affected?

The people who are affected can be students, families, social media users, and more. People who believe false information may also share it to others, causing it to spread.

Causes

What are the causes of misinformation?

  • People share information without checking the source.
  • Fake accounts and edited media spread false claims.
  • Social media algorithms can spread popular or attention-grabbing content quickly.
  • Some people create fake information to gain attention or influence others.
  • People may lack media and information literacy skills.

Relevance and Importance

Why is it relevant and important?

Misinformation or Fake Media is relevant and important because it can affect people's decisions and actions. It can also cause confusion, fear, and harm to people. Learning how to identify reliable information helps prevent its spread.
