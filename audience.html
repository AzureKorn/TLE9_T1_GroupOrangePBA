Audience

Who is our target audience?

Our target audience is students, families, and social media users. These people are affected by misinformation or fake media because they may believe false information and share it to others without checking if it's true or factual.

Average Target Audience

The average target audience of our chosen problem is people who are 13-35 years old and are active on social media platforms. They can hardly tell whether the information is true or not, causing them to believe in false claims and spreading it to others. Most of them are students, families, and regular internet users who can easily be influenced by misinformation.

Youtube Category

Of all the YouTube categories available, we will focus on "News & Politics". A lot of people in this category are more exposed to fake news and informations that they can't tell whether or not they're real or fake. If we help them how to spot fake news, the spread of fake news can be reduced.

Examples:

  • Post about a celebrity who died without giving evidence as to what happened.
  • Video claiming that a certain food causes a disease without proof.
  • AI video of a popular person saying something terrible they never said.
